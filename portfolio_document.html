L.U.N.A. — AI Lung Disease Detection

L.U.N.A. (Lung Understanding & Neural Analytics) 2025 – Ongoing

AI-Powered Multi-Modal Lung Disease Detection & Clinical Diagnostic Assistance System

Problem Statement

Pulmonary diseases (Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, COVID-19, and Lung Nodules) remain primary global health threats. Diagnostic bottlenecks in rural radiological clinics, manual X-ray interpretation errors, and delayed acoustic stethoscopic analysis often lead to misdiagnosis and delayed treatment.

Proposed AI Solution

L.U.N.A. introduces a multi-modal deep learning platform combining Chest X-Ray Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with Respiratory Sound Spectrogram Analysis (Audio CNN) to provide dual-channel diagnostic triage with high sensitivity and heat-map interpretability.

Technology Stack & Frameworks
Python 3.10 TensorFlow / Keras OpenCV Librosa (Audio Processing) Scikit-Learn Flask API HTML5/CSS3
Key Multi-Modal System Features
  • Dual-Modal Diagnostics: Processes both DICOM/PNG X-Ray scans and WAV digital stethoscope audio.
  • Grad-CAM Visual Heatmaps: Highlights pathological lung zones directly on radiological images for clinical validation.
  • Acoustic Spectrogram Classification: Converts respiratory wheezes and crackles into Mel-Spectrograms for deep feature extraction.
  • Automated Diagnostic Summary: Generates structured triage reports with confidence probability scores.
